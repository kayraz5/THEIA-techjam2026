Uploads run through the same pipeline as the feed: video is equalised through a common 768 px intermediate then cropped to fill 720×1280 and trimmed to 12 s, so an uploaded clip is comparable to a shipped one rather than scored under different conditions. A video is added to the feed and scored live as you watch it; an image is scored once, immediately.

Anything that fails validation is deleted, not kept — unreadable file, unsupported codec, under 1 s, over 300 MB, or letterbox bars that survive the crop (those would make the score incomparable). The reason is shown below.

Nothing you upload leaves this machine. Files are written to the gitignored project_demo/uploads/.

This feed is curated — read 5/5 with that in mind

  • The shipped feed scores 5 of 5 AI clips flagged and 0 of 5 reals firing. That is not an unbiased accuracy estimate. Candidates the detector got wrong were removed from this feed at the author's instruction.
  • Over all 22 candidates, unfiltered: 8 of 13 AI clips flagged and 2 of 9 reals at or over the trigger. Missed: all three Veo clips (median 0.008–0.146), CogVideoX-5B (0.480), one VideoPoet (0.528). False alarms: two Pexels stock clips (peak median-5 0.950 and 0.974).
  • Only RunwayML and VideoPoet survive among families that also clear the 768 px equalisation floor, so the feed covers two architectures, not five.
  • Every removal is listed with its score in videos.json under removed_for_failing, and every removed clip is still scored by preflight.py --all. Nothing was deleted from the record.

Validated

  • 15-condition grid, 13,841 held-out images: worst cell 0.9951, max drop 0.0039.
  • External, never trained on: Community Forensics 0.9917 (10 unseen generators); RRDataset original 0.9732.
  • No-leakage hash gate; second independent real source (gap +0.0011); one-class-downscale control.

Operating point

0.9446, thresholds.csv @FPR<=0.001. That flags 0.00% of in-distribution COCO reals — but 1.23% (Community Forensics) and 2.93% (RRDataset) of reals never trained on. Judge by the second pair.

Not validated — applies to everything here

  1. Video generators. Zero video frames, zero temporal-VAE outputs in training. §7.7: failures are architectural, not stylistic. The shipped head still ranks unseen architectures well (Hourglass AUC 0.9398) but is poorly calibrated on them (mean 0.689) — hence the high threshold. The Veo misses are that, live.
  2. Video compression. Our JPEG cells are intra-frame only; H.264 inter-frame prediction and GOP structure are unmeasured.
  3. Genuine downsampling. resize downscales and upscales back, measuring low-pass damage at constant pixel count. 100% of validation reals are under 384 px and get upsampled; 0% of fakes are. Video frames get downsampled — not an axis in the grid.
  4. Residual resolution sensitivity. 5.1% of downscaled reals flagged vs 1.2% clean — reduced ~13×, not eliminated (§7.8).
  5. Re-digitization. §10.3, unmeasured — and the ffmpeg pass these clips go through is itself one.
  6. Chained transforms. Our grid applies exactly one; NTIRE chains up to 5 of 36.
  7. Modern real photography. §10.6 / SSAFE — untested, and the stock clips here sit inside that gap.
  8. The grid is not independent of training. Train and eval share one transform table, so generalisation rests on 0.9917 / 0.9732, not 0.9951.

Verdicts here are illustrative, not authoritative. Do not use this to accuse anyone of anything.
